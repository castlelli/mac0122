# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students of MAC0122 (Princípios de Desenvolvimento de Algoritmos, IME-USP) who
want to find and review what the course studies: an algorithm, the technique
behind it, who created it, when, which concepts it uses, and which problem it
solves. They read on a laptop or a phone, often while studying for an exam or
writing an exercise.

## Product Purpose

EP1 for MAC0122: a C89 program reads the course-content TXT files and generates
a static site that centralizes the course material. Success is a reader
reaching any algorithm page from any of the six indexes in a few clicks, and
reading its explanation and its syntax-highlighted C code comfortably.

## Positioning

The site is built from the course's own TXTs through six indexes (technique,
algorithm, authors, date, concepts, problem), each sortable by every
single-valued attribute it does not group by. The same material is reachable
from whichever angle the reader starts with.

## Operating Context

- Generated by `./ep1 input/*.txt` into `output/`; opened from disk or served as
  static files, possibly offline.
- The TXTs are written in Portuguese by the course staff and the group;
  `input/txt-format.pt-BR.md` is their guide.
- The course is taught by Prof. Dr. Leônidas de Oliveira Brandão (confirmed by
  the user for the masthead).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML and one shared `style.css`; no JavaScript, no images, no external
  requests (no web fonts).
- The generator is C89: CSS lives in string literals of at most 509 characters.
- Output must be byte-identical across runs, so pages carry no dates or run
  data.
- Index keys group and link in normalized form (lowercase, no accents), but
  are shown as the first TXT by file name spells them, capitalized; see
  README.md.
- Code colors come from the C highlighter and are not changed by the site
  design.
- A "Grafo" tab shows the whole database as a graph of pages and keys
  (global view) and one local graph per page or key, still as static
  HTML and SVG with no JavaScript.

## Brand Commitments

- Text-only identity: "MAC0122", "Princípios de Desenvolvimento de Algoritmos",
  "IME-USP", and the professor's name. No logos or official USP visual
  identity, since this is a student project, not an official USP page.
- The footer credits the authors as "Construído por Alunos da Escola
  Politécnica da Universidade de São Paulo" and links the repository
  (https://github.com/castlelli/mac0122) as "GitHub".
- Language of the pages: Portuguese (pt-BR).
- Tone: simple, professional, institutional. No fluff, no dashboards.

## Evidence on Hand

- The course's "Objetivos" sentence from JupiterWeb, used on the home page with
  its source named.
- Course content exists only as TXTs; `tests/fixtures/site/` holds test pages,
  not course content. Nothing else (testimonials, statistics) may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The reader's question leads: every page shows where the reader is and how to
   reach the other indexes.
2. Content over chrome: the indexes and the code are the substance.
3. Works anywhere: offline, without JavaScript, at phone width, and in print.
4. Deterministic and honest: the site shows only what the TXTs and the
   confirmed course facts say.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard navigation with visible focus, semantic
landmarks, and a skip link.
